# R14 — both remaining side-panel tabs replaced by bolts

R13 retained two thin passive locators on **P15B**. R14 removes their projecting tabs and replaces them with **H7 (front)** and **H8 (rear)**: reinforced 5 mm lap ears, matching 3.6 mm clamp pads, and M3 bolts/nuts. The new nuts use the same tapered PETG retention ribs as R13.

**Update all three parts together: P15B, P15C and P15D.** P15C/P15D now contain the matching bolt holes and receivers. Keep all other printed robot parts and existing hardware. Add **two M3×10 socket-head screws, two standard M3 nuts and two M3 washers**. Do not use M3×12 at H7/H8; the longer front screw contacts the sensor frame.
