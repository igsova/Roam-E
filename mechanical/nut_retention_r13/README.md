# R13 — glue-free hood nut retention (PETG prototype)

R13 adds six small tapered press-in ribs to each of the **H1–H6** M3 hex nut pockets. The ribs are intended to hold the nuts while handling and assembling the covers; the hex walls prevent rotation, and the existing pocket shoulders carry the clamping load. This is an interference fit, not a snap latch or a certified retaining-force design.

Only **P15B (removable side panel)** and **P15D (rear hood)** change from R12. Keep the existing R12 P15C front smiling hood and all other printed robot components. No extra bolts, nuts or washers are required. R12 and earlier files have not been overwritten.

**Print the three `Q13*_standard` samples before the covers.** Each is cropped from an actual joint and retains its cover's print-layer orientation. Light and firm alternatives are supplied for calibration. The delivered covers use the standard profile; if a different sample works better, regenerate the covers to match before printing them. Do not scale the whole model to change nut fit.

The flat roof, attached face, reinforced 5 mm side laps and horizontal assembly paths remain unchanged. Local print supports may still be necessary. Physical grip, insertion force, repeated-use life and PETG print accuracy have not been tested.
